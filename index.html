How Thabt works

One underlying mechanism, built to serve every segment the same way.

01

Register

Upload any document. Thabt generates a unique cryptographic fingerprint of its exact content.

02

Record

That fingerprint, with a timestamp, is written to the blockchain as permanent, tamper-evident proof.

03

Verify

Anytime, anyone can check a document against its registered fingerprint — a match confirms nothing changed.

04

Deploy your way

Individuals use Thabt's cloud platform. Institutions can run it entirely on their own servers.
